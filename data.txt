Core Subjects: Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, English, Second Language
Grade 11-12 Streams:
  - Science: Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Computer Science, Math, English
  - Commerce: Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics, Math/Informatics Practices, English
  - Arts/Humanities: History, Political Science, Psychology, Economics, English
